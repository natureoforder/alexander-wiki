# The Oregon Experiment

<img src="/assets/covers/1975-the-oregon-experiment.jpg" alt="The Oregon Experiment book cover" class="works-cover" />

1975 · Christopher Alexander, Murray Silverstein, Shlomo Angel, Sara Ishikawa and Denny Abrams · Oxford University Press · ISBN 0-19-501824-9

## What it is

The planning process Alexander and his colleagues proposed for the University of Oregon in Eugene, in place of a conventional master plan. Instead of a fixed drawing of the future campus, the university would grow through many small projects designed by their users, guided by a shared set of patterns and by an annual diagnosis of what was working and what was not. The book sets out six principles for this process: organic order, participation, piecemeal growth, patterns, diagnosis and coordination.

## Why it matters

The third volume of the series that includes *The Timeless Way of Building* and *A Pattern Language*, and the first to show a pattern language used by a real institution. It is Alexander's earliest full account of planning as a process of growth rather than a blueprint, an idea he returned to in *A New Theory of Urban Design* and *The Nature of Order*, Book 2.
