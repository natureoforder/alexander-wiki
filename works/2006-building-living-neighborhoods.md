# Building Living Neighborhoods

c. 2004–2006 · Center for Environmental Structure and Centre for Environmental Structure, Europe · website, livingneighborhoods.org (pages carry a 2006 copyright)

## What it is

A website setting out a practical method for laying out and building a neighborhood with the people who will live and work there. Its core is a *generative code*: six phases of steps, or *unfoldings* (startup and vision, diagnosis of the land, setting density, modifying the code, public spaces, building volumes), worked through on the land itself. Around it sit a library of further unfoldings, guidance on running a project (contracts, developers, construction), example projects such as Eishin, Mexicali, West Dean and Strood, theory linked to *The Nature of Order*, and papers including drafts of the generative codes book.

## Why it matters

It is the most practical statement of the late work: *The Nature of Order*'s idea of living process turned into steps a group can follow for a real site. It is the backbone for this wiki's guidance on building with Alexander.
