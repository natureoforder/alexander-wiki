<header class="noo-head">
<h1 class="noo-title">The Nature of Order<span>a way in, in pictures</span></h1>
<p class="noo-lede">Christopher Alexander (1936–2022) spent fifty years asking why some places make us feel more alive. <em>The Nature of Order</em> (2002–2005), four books and over two thousand pages, is his final answer. Here it is in eight questions, each opening the next, told mostly through the pictures he chose.</p>
</header>

<div class="noo-books">
<a class="noo-book" href="/works/2002-the-phenomenon-of-life"><img src="/assets/covers/2002-the-phenomenon-of-life.jpg" alt="Cover of The Phenomenon of Life" /><span class="noo-mono">Book 1 · Life</span></a>
<a class="noo-book" href="/works/2002-the-process-of-creating-life"><img src="/assets/covers/2002-the-process-of-creating-life.jpg" alt="Cover of The Process of Creating Life" /><span class="noo-mono">Book 2 · Process</span></a>
<a class="noo-book" href="/works/2005-a-vision-of-a-living-world"><img src="/assets/covers/2005-a-vision-of-a-living-world.jpg" alt="Cover of A Vision of a Living World" /><span class="noo-mono">Book 3 · Vision</span></a>
<a class="noo-book" href="/works/2004-the-luminous-ground"><img src="/assets/covers/2004-the-luminous-ground.jpg" alt="Cover of The Luminous Ground" /><span class="noo-mono">Book 4 · Ground</span></a>
</div>

<ol class="noo-spine">
<li><a href="#q1">What makes some places good? <em>They feel alive, and life comes in degrees.</em></a></li>
<li><a href="#q2">Where else do we know that feeling? <em>In living things.</em></a></li>
<li><a href="#q3">What is that life made of? <em>Centers, helping each other.</em></a></li>
<li><a href="#q4">How does nature make it? <em>Step by step, each step adding to the whole.</em></a></li>
<li><a href="#q5">Can we build that way? <em>Yes: by the same kind of unfolding.</em></a></li>
<li><a href="#q6">What does it look like? <em>Buildings he and his colleagues made.</em></a></li>
<li><a href="#q7">Then why is it so rare? <em>Our whole system builds another way.</em></a></li>
<li><a href="#q8">What lies underneath? <em>A ground we share with everything.</em></a></li>
</ol>

<h2 class="noo-step__q">What is that life made of?</h2>

<h2 class="noo-step__q">Can we build that way?</h2>
<p class="noo-step__a">Yes, by the same kind of process: a sequence of steps, each one making or strengthening a center, each one judged by feeling, on site. Patterns become rules for making centers, and sequences say in what order to use them.</p>

<h2 class="noo-step__q">What lies underneath?</h2>
<p class="noo-step__a">In the last book Alexander goes furthest. The more alive a thing is, he argues, the more it is like a self, and through it we touch something self-like that underlies everything, which he calls the “I” or the luminous ground. Here he is at his most personal and most contested.</p>
