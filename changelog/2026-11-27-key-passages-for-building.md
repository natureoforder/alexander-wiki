Three passages from *The Nature of Order* that matter most for actually building, now on the site in Alexander's own words with every photograph and drawing, and our introductions set apart in a box:

- [Building the Upham house](https://alexander.wiki/practice/upham-house): the whole appendix of *The Process of Creating Life*, forty-four steps from finding the site to planting the garden, each step linkable.
- [Laying out a hamlet or a campus](https://alexander.wiki/practice/eishin-campus-reading): his method at Eishin, from balsa models and two hundred flags in the fields to the 1:200 model in clay and manila card.
- [Ten tacit assumptions of the modern worldview](https://alexander.wiki/notes/ten-tacit-assumptions): his list, from *The Luminous Ground*, of the hidden premises that make value seem subjective, with his notes.
