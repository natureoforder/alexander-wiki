# Front page mockups (26 Nov), not used

Three openings tried for the front page: A, Contents; B, Plates; C, The question. They were published on the preview at `/mockups/front-a`, `front-b` and `front-c`, generated by `build.py` with `mockups.css` appended to the preview's `custom.css`.

Rufus's verdict: all three were worse than the existing front page, because they were "denser and more overloaded". Six ways in instead of two, and more text and pictures. We kept the original and made only small fixes: a bigger title, more space under it, and a roman subtitle.

Worth keeping: B's four black and white plates (Window Place, the Isfahan porch, the Eishin flags, mud cracks), which Rufus called "very powerful". They might be used further down a page, or elsewhere. The Anatolian carpet, in colour and on its own, is an idea for a touch of colour. Both are unplanned.
