# Concept list

Drawn from the six research digests (`natureoforder/sources/texts/*/digest.md`), the rebuild plan and the roadmap candidates. The roadmap takes its stops from this list. Abbreviations: NoO 1–4 = *The Nature of Order* Books One to Four; TWB = *The Timeless Way of Building*; APL = *A Pattern Language*. Page numbers are printed pages as given in the digests. Every quotation below is copied from a digest, which in turn is from machine-draft text: check each against the scan before it goes on a public page.

## 1. Principles used to choose

- **Concept page**: an idea Alexander argues for over a chapter or more, which other ideas depend on, and which can carry three to eight excerpts. 49 pages in all.
- **Section of another page**: an idea he develops only inside a larger one (latent centers, differentiation), or a list whose items get a page or less each (the fifteen transformations, the eleven color properties).
- **Glossary entry**: a term a reader will meet and search for, pointing to the page where it lives.
- **Works, Build with Alexander, Patterns, Readings**: books, projects, individual patterns and scale-specific procedures (how to site a house, the contract) are not concepts.
- **Naming**: Alexander's own term where he has a good one, in his spelling (center, color); plain English where his term is opaque or missing, marked "ours".
- **One idea, one page**, even when he renamed it across books; the page tells the history.

## Revised after critique (26 Nov) — proposed to Rufus

An independent review argued, and we agree, that two pairs in the draft ten overlap and that the ten had no social side. Proposed core ten, in reading order:

1. **Life (the quality without a name)** `quality-without-a-name` — merges the felt quality (TWB) and degrees of life (NoO 1); Alexander links them himself (NoO 1 p. 36).
2. **Wholeness** `wholeness`
3. **Centers** `centers`
4. **The fifteen properties** `fifteen-properties`
5. **The mirror of the self** `mirror-of-the-self` — moved after the properties, as in NoO 1 (ch. 8): the test reads better once you know what it tests for.
6. **Structure-preserving transformations** `structure-preserving-transformations` — his precise, operational term; "unfolding wholeness" becomes its opening section and a glossary entry.
7. **Living process** `living-process`
8. **Pattern language** `pattern-language`
9. **Belonging** `belonging` — who builds and for whom; the moral centre of TWB, APL and NoO 3.
10. **The "I"** `the-i` — last, keeping his own doubt.

Other changes: "Making" → **The architect-builder**; "Value as fact" → **Value is objective**; **Semilattice ("A City is Not a Tree")** added as a main page; Step-by-step adaptation folds into Living process; Making wholeness heals the maker folds into Being alive, The goal of tears into Deep feeling, Beings into The "I"; patterns of events opens the Patterns page. ### Naming choices to weigh

- **1 and 2, "the quality without a name" and "life".** These are one idea in two vocabularies: NoO 1 p. 36 points back to TWB chs. 1–2 for it. Recommended: keep both pages, because they do different jobs. The first is the felt, unnameable quality (and the reader's way in); the second is the 2002 claim that it is a measurable degree. Alternative: one page, "Life (the quality without a name)". For 2, "Degrees of life" (his chapter title) is preferred to "Life", which is too bare as a page title, and to "Living structure", which is a separate main page (what a thing is like when its centers help each other).
- **4 and 5, "wholeness" and "centers".** Recommended: two pages, and retire the third (`wholeness-and-centers`). He defines the two terms separately (pp. 81, 84) and "centers" is already a pilot page. Alternative: one page, "Wholeness and centers", after his chapter title; that would free a core slot for Belonging.
- **7, "unfolding wholeness" or "structure-preserving transformations".** Recommended: the plain word leads, matching the roadmap's starred stop; this page also holds differentiation and generated-versus-fabricated. "Structure-preserving transformations" stays as a main page (the single step, latent centers, the fifteen transformations). Alternative: make the technical term the core one, since everything in NoO 2 is defined through it.
- **8, "living process" or "the fundamental process".** One page. "Living process" is the class he defines; "the fundamental process" is the single repeated step at its heart and becomes the page's main section and a glossary entry.
- **9, "pattern language" or "patterns".** Two pages; the language is the core one, because the claim that matters is that patterns work only as a shared language. "Patterns" (what one pattern is) is a main page.
- **10, The "I".** His own term, awkward in a title and a URL. Alternatives: "The ground", "The luminous ground", "The eternal self". Recommended: The "I", with the others as glossary entries pointing to it.
- **Nearest misses.** Belonging (the roadmap's act IV needs a starred page; see open question 4) and Deep feeling.
