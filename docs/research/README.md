# Research digests

One compact digest per source book, built chapter by chapter from `natureoforder/sources/texts/`. They feed the concept list, the roadmap and the concept pages. Not published (`docs/` is excluded from the site). Finished digests live in the sources repo beside their book, for future AI and research use.

## Digest shape

1. **The book in brief**: what it argues and how it fits Alexander's project (≤200 words).
2. **The argument, chapter by chapter**: for each chapter, the claim in 2–4 sentences, its main examples, and the 1–2 passages that best state it in his words (verbatim, ≤60 words each, with printed page).
3. **Key concepts**: table of name · his own defining phrase (verbatim, page) · where it is developed (chapter, pages) · related concepts.
4. **Figures worth using**: the drawings and photographs that carry the argument (chapter, page, what they show, figure file if extracted).
5. **Links to the other books**: where an idea starts, is revised, or is superseded elsewhere.
6. **Stories and cases**: projects, buildings and anecdotes he uses (Eishin, Mexicali, the fishpond, the window seat…), with pages.
7. **Text quality notes**: OCR problems met that matter for quoting.
