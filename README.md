# Christopher Alexander Wiki

A concept-first guide to Christopher Alexander, with collections for Living Neighborhoods and A Pattern Language. Start at the [wiki homepage](index.md).

The repository root is the Flowershow content root. Keep research transcriptions in the separate sources workspace and cite the published edition and chapter or pattern number from wiki pages.

Project scope and dependencies live in the Beads epic `alex-88s`. Run `bd show alex-88s` from a bootstrapped clone.

## Flowershow preview

[Open the preview](https://alexander-wiki-preview-rufuspollock.flowershow.me). The preview uses the Flowershow CLI under the `rufuspollock` account. Its local publishing directory is `~/.cache/alexander-wiki-flowershow-preview/`. The production site will publish from this GitHub repository.

## Design and sources

The reading design takes its spacing, small-cap titles, and old-style numerals from the interior of *A Pattern Language*, pp. 833–834. Pattern readings pair a plate with commentary on wide screens and put the plate first on phones. The side-by-side comparison is kept at [about/design.md](about/design.md), published at `/about/design`.

The navbar and favicon use the cover emblem from Christopher Alexander, *The Nature of Order*, Book One, *The Phenomenon of Life* (Center for Environmental Structure, 2002). The mark is isolated from the front jacket scan on a transparent background.

The collection includes the dust jacket of Christopher Alexander and colleagues’ *A Pattern Language* (Oxford University Press, 1977, ISBN 9780195019193). [Jacket image](https://images-na.ssl-images-amazon.com/images/P/0195019199.01.L.jpg)

For a passage citation, put a numbered Markdown footnote such as `[^1]` beside the passage and its definition, `[^1]: Citation`, after the article. Short notes numbered 1–9 can sit beside their paragraphs on wide screens. Longer sequences continue as endnotes.
