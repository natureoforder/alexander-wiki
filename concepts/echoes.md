# Echoes

A roof, a porch opening, and a gate can share a family resemblance even when they differ in size and serve different purposes. Alexander calls these recurrences echoes. An angle, a curve, or a proportion appears again, and the later encounter changes how you see the first.[^1]

Choose two details at opposite ends of a building. Sketch each in three strokes. Do the sketches have a common gesture? Look for a third occurrence at another scale.

See also [levels of scale](levels-of-scale.md) and [the fifteen properties](fifteen-properties.md).

[^1]: Christopher Alexander, *The Nature of Order*, Book One, *The Phenomenon of Life*, chapter 5, pp. 218–221.
