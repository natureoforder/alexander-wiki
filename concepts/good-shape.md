# Good shape

A shape can be easy to grasp as a whole while rewarding a closer look. Alexander follows its outline inward: the tip, shoulder, or opening that gives the whole its character can itself resolve into smaller, equally definite shapes.[^1]

Trace the silhouette of a chair from the side. Look again at the bend between back and seat, then at the open shape between two legs. Which of those smaller shapes contributes most to the first outline you saw?

See also [positive space](positive-space.md) and [the fifteen properties](fifteen-properties.md).

[^1]: Christopher Alexander, *The Nature of Order*, Book One, *The Phenomenon of Life*, chapter 5, pp. 179–185.
