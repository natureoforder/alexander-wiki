# Local symmetries

A street can bend with the land while individual windows, gates and bays each have a recognizable balance of their own. These local symmetries let the parts hold together while the larger arrangement responds to a different set of conditions.[^1]

On a walk, look for the shortest stretch of frontage that has a center line. Then find a larger group of openings with another center line. Where the two groups overlap, look for what belongs to both.

See also [strong centers](strong-centers.md) and [the fifteen properties](fifteen-properties.md).

[^1]: Christopher Alexander, *The Nature of Order*, Book One, *The Phenomenon of Life*, chapter 5, pp. 186–194.
