# Patterns

One corner of the porch had to go. Its builder had laid out a platform for sitting in the sun, but people already walked across that corner on their way to the front door. He trimmed the platform to give the path its turn. The idea that helped him see what to do was *Paths and Goals*, a pattern usually applied to routes through a neighborhood.[^1]

A **pattern** names a design problem that turns up in many places and describes a relationship that can resolve it. A pattern language connects these proposals so that decisions made at one scale help with decisions at another. The porch builder chose ten patterns. *Outdoor Room* suggested some overhead shelter. A mature tree already stood where he wanted to sit, so he kept it and let its branches extend the shelter of the existing roof.[^2]
