# The void

In the prayer rug Alexander discusses, a deep blue open field holds the middle while more elaborate forms gather around it. The patterned surround helps that emptiness take on a presence of its own.[^1]

Look at a table crowded around its edges but clear in the middle. Imagine adding one more object there. Does it complete the arrangement, or does it take away the still place that gave the surrounding objects room?

See also [strong centers](strong-centers.md) and [the fifteen properties](fifteen-properties.md).

[^1]: Christopher Alexander, *The Nature of Order*, Book One, *The Phenomenon of Life*, chapter 5, pp. 222–225.
