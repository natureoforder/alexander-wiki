# Alternating repetition

An arcade repeats two things: the columns and the openings between them. Walk along it and watch one come forward, then the other. The openings have their own proportions, light and uses. Alexander calls attention to the way two series of centers can sustain each other.[^1]

Try drawing only the gaps between the uprights of a fence. Does the drawing reveal a second rhythm you could recognize on its own? Alexander finds this kind of alternation at scales ranging from woven strands to the bays of a building.
