# Christopher Alexander: Second Renaissance Visionary

A talk by Rufus Pollock to the Building Beauty group, for an audience who know Alexander well: where his work sits in a larger shift in what we take to be real, how we know, and what matters. It is the more recent and fuller of his two talks on Alexander, and supersedes the [2024 talk](2024-talk-pioneer-of-the-second-renaissance.md).
