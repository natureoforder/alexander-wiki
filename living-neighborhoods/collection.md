# Explore the Living Neighborhoods collection

Choose an entry point below to read on the original site. The workbook organizes activities for a group working on a particular site. The project accounts show what happened when people tried these ideas in particular places.

## Working with a group

- [Action and practice](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/action.htm) introduces the practical materials.
- [The neighborhood workbook](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/workbook.htm) assembles the work in phases. Enter at [startup and vision](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/packets/vision-phase.htm), [diagnosis](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/packets/diagnosis-phase.htm), or [public spaces](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/packets/publicspaces-phase.htm).
- [The library of unfoldings](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/libraryofunfoldings.htm) collects individual steps. Two examples concern [walking routes](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/actions/pedestriansfirst.htm) and [the shape of outdoor space](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/actions/positivespace.htm).

## Places

- [Fresno](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/fresno.htm) follows the life of a farmers’ market.
- [Mexicali](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/mexicali.htm) shows housing built with the participating families.
- [Lake Travis](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/texas.htm) looks at the land held in common among a group of homes.
- [Eishin](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/eishincampus.htm) follows the making of a school campus in Japan.

## Ideas and papers

- [Theory and examples](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/theory.htm) brings the authors’ arguments and examples together.
- [The Nature of Order](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/natureoforder.htm) relates the collection to the four volumes.
- [The authors’ archive](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/ht-0/archive.htm) lists papers, manuals, and drafts.
