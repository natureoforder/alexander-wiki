# About this wiki

Christopher Alexander (1936–2022) was the architect and thinker behind *A Pattern Language*, *The Timeless Way of Building* and *The Nature of Order*. He and his collaborators spent their lives asking why some places feel alive and others do not, and how ordinary people could make places of the first kind. Their answers reach well past architecture. They take in how we see, how things grow, and what feeling and value are. This wiki is a way into those ideas, set out in his own words wherever possible.

Most of his books and nearly all his buildings were made with others, among them Sara Ishikawa, Murray Silverstein, Max Jacobson, Ingrid Fiksdahl-King, Shlomo Angel, Howard Davis, Hajo Neis, Gary Black, Artemis Anninou, Randy Schmidt and Maggie Moore Alexander. When we write "Alexander" we mean him and the people he worked with, and each work's page names its authors.

## What it is for

- **The vision.** Alexander as a philosopher and pioneer as well as an architect: a different picture of matter, life, value and the self.
- **The ideas.** His central concepts, each with passages from the books, page references and his own drawings and photographs.
- **The practice.** How to actually build this way, from a room to a house to a hamlet. We came to Alexander partly because we want to do this ourselves.

It is not a catalogue of Alexander's buildings or a complete archive of his work. For those, see the [Christopher Alexander CES Archive](https://christopher-alexander-ces-archive.org/) and [PatternLanguage.com](https://www.patternlanguage.com/).

## How it is made

Each concept page pairs a short summary in our own words with passages from Alexander's books. Every passage cites the book, chapter and printed page, and is checked against the page itself. Where we give an example of our own, we say so.
